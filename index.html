Theory, step by step

Spatial beat

For aligned gratings, the beat frequency is the magnitude of the frequency difference.

f_M = |f_1 - f_2|

What is aliasing?

Aliasing is a false lower-frequency pattern that appears when a repeating signal is sampled too coarsely to represent it uniquely.

f_alias = f - m*f_s

Nyquist

A sampling grid with frequency f_s has a fundamental limit at half that frequency.

f_N = f_s / 2
